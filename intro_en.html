Product Overview

The YQ2401-05 Vortex Mixer is a sample mixing device designed for laboratory use. It uses high-speed vortex motion to create a vigorous whirlpool inside tubes, achieving rapid and uniform mixing results. With easy operation, stable performance, and wide applicability, it is extensively used in various experimental procedures across biology, chemistry, clinical diagnostics, and environmental monitoring.

From DNA/RNA extraction, PCR reagent mixing, ELISA experiments, and drug solubility testing to everyday reagent reconstitution and resuspension, the YQ2401-05 delivers efficient and reliable support.

Key Features

✓

Dual Operating Modes

Supports both touch (momentary) and continuous operating modes to suit different operating habits and experimental needs.

✓

Stepless Speed Control

Knob-based stepless speed adjustment with a wide speed range, allowing flexible mixing intensity control based on sample characteristics.

Multiple Adapter Heads

Optional adapter heads in various sizes, compatible with tubes and containers from 0.2 mL to 50 mL.

Smooth, Low-Noise Operation

Premium motor and vibration-damping design minimize vibration and noise, so it never disturbs work even in quiet environments.

✓

Long-Lasting Durability

High-strength engineering plastic or alloy housing with a compact structure, long service life, and low maintenance costs.

✓

Compact & Portable

Small footprint and lightweight, taking up minimal bench space and easy to move and store.
